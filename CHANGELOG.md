## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-25

### Added
- First release: one theme with light mode (day map) and dark mode (night map).
- Contour background as an embedded SVG, white map sheets with a thin forest-green rim, no shadows. Works without add-ons.
- Five map inks only (forest green, contour brown, water blue, road red, ink); every palette colour of the frontend is mapped onto them. Text 4.5:1 and controls 3:1 in both modes.
- Road red for active devices, forest-green switches, selection and keyboard focus, narrow system fonts.
- Optional card-mod rules: red map pins on active device tiles, coloured lights and badges in road red, dashed rules between rows of entities cards, letter-spaced capitals for titles and the sidebar, map font and framed selection in the sidebar.
